privacy policy

Good Company ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights, in plain language.

what we collect

When you submit your email address through our founding member form, we collect:

  • Your email address

We do not collect your name, payment information, or any other personal data at this stage. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools that identify you individually.

how we use it

Your email address is used solely to:

  • Confirm your place on the founding member list
  • Send you information about upcoming Good Company dinners in Toronto
  • Communicate changes to the program

We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

how we store it

Form submissions are handled through Netlify Forms and stored securely on Netlify's infrastructure. Netlify is SOC 2 Type II certified. You can read their privacy policy at netlify.com/privacy.

your rights

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable Ontario privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Withdraw your consent and request deletion of your data at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

withdrawing consent

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any message we send, or by contacting us directly. We will remove your information from our list promptly.

contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at hello@goodcompanyclub.io.

changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
